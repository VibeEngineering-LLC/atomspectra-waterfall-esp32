## Highlights

**#FW-8 residual — hist `sweep dropped` metronome**

LittleFS autosave (~33 KiB) and waterfall segment flash starved the USB FTDI FIFO in the post-commit quiet window → staging gaps → intentional #FW-8 drops (~70/h with waterfall off, ~80/h with waterfall on).

**Fix (default `HIST_DROP_I3_SLICED=1`):** shared quiet-budget helper; sliced autosave (`current.bin.tmp` → rename) with soft **yield** on commit-wait×3 (tmp kept) and **force one-shot** after `fail_streak≥5`; offline USB → one-shot; skip live-USB **batch** row `fsync`, but **`seg_finalize` always fsyncs**; waterfall baseline/rows sliced; quiet gap after finalize; quiet `unlink` with success check (`make_room` cannot spin); writer-lock 500 ms (no segment drop on contention). Telemetry: `autosave_age_sec` / `autosave_fail_streak` on `/api/spectrum.json`.

Write-up: [`docs/bugs/2026-08-12-histogram-sweep-drops-autosave.md`](../bugs/2026-08-12-histogram-sweep-drops-autosave.md).

> Note: #FW-51 / #FW-43 CDC reconnect hardening already shipped in **v1.2.5** (upstream PRs #17 / #18). This release is the hist quiet-budget follow-on.
